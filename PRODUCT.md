# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

small business owners and their customers who need friction-free appointment scheduling

## Product Purpose

Eliminate the back-and-forth of appointment booking with intelligent conflict detection

## Brand Personality

friendly, efficient, trustworthy

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates with purple-to-blue gradients
- Over-designed dashboards where data is buried in decoration
- AI-generated interfaces that all share one look (Inter font, cream backgrounds, rounded everything)
- Enterprise apps that confuse density with professionalism

## Design Principles

- Book in seconds, not minutes
- Trust through transparency (availability, pricing, policies)
- Dual interface: customer simplicity, owner power
- Never double-book, never over-commit

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance target
- Proper color contrast ratios (body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1)
- Keyboard navigable interface
- Screen reader friendly semantic HTML
- Support for prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme
